# Explanation about Turtle runaway

**Timer**
A 30-second timer is displayed at the top left.
When the timer runs out, a "Game Over" message and the score are displayed.

**Smart Turtle**
It moves randomly under normal circumstances, but when the Chaser approaches, it evades in the opposite direction.
When approaching the edge of the screen, the character dodges toward the center to avoid going off-screen.

**Score system**
The chaser earns 10 points each time they catch a runner. A caught runner is teleported to a random location and can continue playing for the remainder of the time limit.
